## Official Rules (key sections)
- §1 Sponsor: Coinbase Technologies, Inc. ("Base"). Partner Tracks under §8.
- §2 Period: Nov 5 – Dec 5, 2026; submissions must be received in-period; Sponsor's clock is official; times UTC unless page says otherwise.
- §3 Tracks: the 3 above, judged/awarded separately. Track Sections (on Quest Page) describe each track, give example ideas and "Show us" guidance, set prizes. Quest Page is part of the Rules.
- §4 Eligibility: 18+; not resident in OFAC-sanctioned countries/regions; Base employees/family/household excluded. Teams ≤5, one Team Representative (prize paid to them).
- §5 How to enter: (1) public X post with video demo (e.g. Loom) explaining the project, tagging @buildonbase; (2) official submission form (link, track(s), other requested info); (3) any Track Section requirements. Submission complete only when Sponsor receives the form. No purchase/transaction/wallet funding/token holding/payment required. Testnet allowed, not judged less favourably. One submission per track per entrant/team; one project may win in only one track unless page says otherwise.
- §6 Submission requirements: built on / meaningfully integrates Base + fits track; original work (pre-existing code/products OK if form clearly states what was built during the Quest Period); no IP/privacy infringement; no malware/obscene/hateful/defamatory/harassing/discriminatory material; no unnecessary personal info; comply with all applicable law (securities, commodities, derivatives, lending, consumer protection, money transmission, gaming, prediction markets, sanctions, data protection); no misrepresentation of endorsement by Sponsor/Partner/issuer/public company; no claiming rights in an underlying public company (e.g. voting rights) the asset doesn't provide.
- §7 Judging: Sponsor screens eligibility/compliance; panel chosen by Sponsor (may include Sponsor personnel, Partner reps for Partner Tracks, ecosystem participants). **"The judges will score each Submission against the criteria included on the Quest Page, weighted equally unless the Quest Page sets different weights."** Decisions final and binding. If <3 eligible submissions in a track, Sponsor may award fewer prizes.
- §8 Partner Tracks: posted on Quest Page with partner identity + prizes; prizes funded/awarded by the Partner; winners chosen using the Judging Criteria; partner may require extra terms for non-monetary benefits. A submission may win both a Track prize and a Partner Track prize unless page says otherwise.
- §9 Winner notification/verification: email / X DM / form contact; respond + return Winner Documents within 7 days (ID/age/residence proof, sanctions screening, W-9/W-8, wallet address, signed eligibility/liability/publicity declaration). Failure = forfeit, alternate chosen.
- §10 Prizes: per-track details in Track Sections; USD amounts, **paid in USDC on Base** unless page says otherwise; delivery normally within 30 days after verification; non-transferable; winners bear taxes; prize ≠ investment/equity/funding commitment.
- §11 IP: entrants keep ownership; Base/affiliates/Partner get a non-exclusive worldwide royalty-free licence for Quest length + 2 years to use/reproduce/display public submission materials (X post, demo, name/logo/descriptions) to administer/judge/promote/report — no right to commercialise underlying code/product. Submissions not confidential.
- §12 Release/limitation of liability; §13 General conditions incl. disqualification for tampering, plagiarism/fraud, **manipulating engagement or traction metrics**, unsportsmanlike conduct; Sponsor may cancel/suspend/modify for fraud/technical failure/law change. §14 No investment/legal/tax advice; track descriptions/example ideas are "for inspiration only." §15–16 + Appendix 1: California law, binding arbitration (AAA), class-action and jury-trial waivers; Coinbase Privacy Policy applies.
